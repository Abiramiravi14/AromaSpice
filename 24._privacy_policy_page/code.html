Privacy Policy

— by Narus Foods —

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This policy explains what information AromaSpice by Narus Foods collects when you browse or shop with us, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to this website and your AromaSpice account.

1. Who we are

This website is operated by Narus Foods Pvt. Ltd. (brand: AromaSpice), Spice Kancheepuram, Tamil Nadu, India 631501. In this policy, "we/us/our" means Narus Foods Pvt. Ltd.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details — name, email address and phone number you provide at registration or in your profile.
  • Order details — delivery address, pincode, items ordered and order history needed to fulfil your orders.
  • Messages — anything you send us through the Contact Us form.
  • Location — only if you click the location button in the header: GPS coordinates are captured to set your delivery area and are kept on your device, never uploaded to us.
  • Device data — standard technical data (browser type, pages visited) used to keep the store running safely.

3. How we use your information

  • To process, pack and deliver your orders and send order/delivery updates.
  • To answer your questions, returns and support requests.
  • To keep you signed in and remember your cart and wishlist.
  • To improve the products, offers and performance of this store.

We never sell your personal information to anyone, and we do not use it for unrelated third-party advertising.

4. Payments

Payments are handled by a certified payment gateway (UPI, NetBanking and cards). Card numbers and PINs are entered on the gateway and are never stored on our servers — we only receive a payment confirmation.

5. Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage to keep you signed in, hold your cart and wishlist per account, and remember your saved delivery address. You can clear this data at any time from your browser settings; doing so will sign you out and empty the guest cart.

6. When we share information

  • Courier partners — your name, address and phone number, only to deliver your order.
  • Payment gateway — the details required to complete and verify your payment.
  • Legal authorities — if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our customers.

7. Data security and retention

Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS) and access inside our systems is limited to what is needed to serve you. We keep account information while your account is active, and longer where tax, warranty or other laws require it.

8. Your rights

You may review and update your details any time in your profile. You may also ask us to correct or delete your personal data, or close your account, by writing to us. We respond to verified requests within a reasonable time.

9. Children

This store is intended for adults (18+) buying household products. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally. The "last updated" date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised; continued use of the store after a change means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact us

Questions about your data or this policy? Email Aromaspice@gmail.com, call +91 9042713404 (Mon–Sat), or use our contact form.
