## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an authentic culinary heritage tailored for premium spice commerce. It communicates purity, farm-to-kitchen freshness, and time-honored artisanal tradition while offering a friction-free transactional experience.

### Visual Movement & Design Style
The system adopts an **Artisanal Heritage Modernism** approach. It balances classical culinary warmth with clean modern utility. Rich, organic warm cream canvases replace stark corporate whites to evoke handmade parchment and raw, earthy farm roots. High-contrast typography pairs an authoritative serif for storytelling and editorial prestige with a geometric, highly legible sans-serif for prices, weights, tabular matrices, and administrative controls.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
Designed for home gourmets, culinary enthusiasts, and health-conscious food lovers who value natural origin, hand-selected ingredients, and zero adulteration. The interface instills trust, appetizing warmth, quiet luxury, and straightforward reliability across both customer storefront and operational admin panels.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration directly from dry Indian spices, terracotta hearths, and lush herbal gardens. 

- **Primary (`#1b4d29` - Coriander Forest Green):** Used for primary conversion triggers, "Add to Cart", primary form submissions, active badges, and successful transaction states. It conveys organic vitality and freshness.
- **Secondary (`#6e1319` - Deep Kashmiri Maroon):** Represents rich spice heritage and premium culinary depth. Applied to announcement top banners, brand headers, editorial subheadings, and administrative mastheads.
- **Tertiary (`#d97706` - Turmeric Amber):** Expresses aromatic energy and warm craftsmanship. Deployed for star ratings, promotional discount tags, warning badges, and accent highlights.
- **Neutral Dark (`#1f2421` - Charcoal Peppercorn):** A deeply saturated organic black used for body text, price indicators, table contents, and form input values to ensure superior contrast without digital sterility.
- **Canvas & Surface Tones:**
  - Base canvas: `#fbf9f4` (Warm Cream) and `#f8f5ee` (Raw Linen).
  - Elevated surfaces: `#ffffff` (Pure White) with framing borders in `#e8e2d5` (Sandstone Neutral).
- **Functional Semantics:**
  - Chilli Crimson (`#dc2626`): Stock alerts, deletion actions, validation errors, and high-impact sale chips.
  - Success Green (`#16a34a`): Completed delivery tags and authenticated checkmarks.

## Typography

The type system blends classical high-contrast serifs with high-efficiency sans-serif letterforms.

- **Display & Section Titles (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for brand moments, major hero value propositions, category showcase headers, and modal titles. Its warm, calligraphic terminals honor gastronomic tradition.
- **Interface & Operational Text (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Handles all transactional touchpoints: product specifications, navigation links, monetary currency symbols (`₹`), SKU descriptions, numeric inputs, data tables, and back-office management rows. Its geometric architecture guarantees crisp legibility under dense informational loads.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to bridge customer catalog browsing and structured administrative management.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Grids
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout with `2rem` gutters and max-width constrained to `1280px` for the storefront and fluid `100%` within admin panels. Outer page margin sits at `3rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` canvas margins. Product grids collapse from 4 columns to 2 or 3 columns.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1rem` edge margins. Administrative tables switch to horizontally scrollable sheets or stacked key-value cards.

### Spacing Scale
Vertical and horizontal rhythm adheres strictly to an 8pt base scale (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 40px). Product grids maintain a `1.5rem` row and column gap, providing adequate breathing room between high-saturation food photography cards.

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve an organic, earthy feel, visual layering avoids excessive digital drop shadows in favor of crisp boundary outlines and tonal elevation.

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Background surfaces bathed in `#fbf9f4` or `#f8f5ee`. Completely flat with zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Surface):** `#ffffff` containers resting on the base canvas. Defined by a delicate structural border of `1px solid #e8e2d5` and a subtle warm ambient shadow: `0 2px 6px -1px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.04), 0 1px 3px -1px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Popovers & Hover States):** Triggered when hovering over product cards or opening filter accordions: border intensifies to `#d9d2c3` alongside an expanded soft shadow: `0 10px 20px -3px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Fixed Navbars):** Header sticky bars and checkout confirmation dialogs employ `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.1), 0 10px 10px -5px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Soft (Level 1)** geometric standard. 

- **Corner Radii:** Base interactive controls (buttons, inputs, dropdown triggers, and filter pills) utilize `0.25rem` (4px) to retain an established, premium, structured silhouette. Larger surfaces like product cards, checkout summarizers, and dashboard modules use `rounded-lg` (0.5rem / 8px).
- **Circular Elements:** Pill-shaped structures are exclusively reserved for status badges (e.g., "Active", "Delivered", "27% OFF") and category thumbnail frames to echo the circular forms of traditional spice containers and spice bowls (*katoris*).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Filled with Forest Green (`#1b4d29`), crisp white text (`#ffffff`), `0.25rem` radius, bold sans-serif label (`label-lg`). Hover state deepens tone to `#143a1f` with subtle transition.
- **Secondary Button:** Outlined with `1.5px solid #1b4d29`, transparent fill, `#1b4d29` text. Hover fills with `#1b4d29` at 8% tint.
- **Accent/Destructive Button:** Maroon (`#6e1319`) for high-urgency promotional triggers ("Buy Now") or Crimson (`#dc2626`) for destructive operational actions.

### Cards & Product Tiles
- Enclosed in `#ffffff` surfaces bounded by `1px solid #e8e2d5`.
- Image frames hold a generous 1:1 square canvas bathed in faint warm ivory (`#faf8f3`) with circular spices bowl displays centered.
- Card footers feature prominent bold pricing (`₹149`), strikethrough MRP (`₹170`), and a micro-percentage badge (`12% OFF` in `#16a34a` or `#dc2626`).

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background `#ffffff`, border `1px solid #dcd5c7`, placeholder `#8a867d`.
- Focus state reveals an outer glow in `#1b4d29` at 20% opacity and a solid `1.5px solid #1b4d29` border ring.
- Global navigation search features integrated category selectors and an attached Forest Green search icon trigger button.

### Badges & Chips
- **Discount & Special Offers:** Amber or Crimson pill containers with white or deep dark text (`label-sm`).
- **Product Weight Selectors (Chips):** Compact rectangular tabs (`50g`, `100g`, `250g`, `500g`, `1kg`) outlined in `#e8e2d5`. Active chip features a filled `#1b4d29` background with white typography.

### Tables & Admin Controls
- Data tables feature `#f8f5ee` header bands with uppercase label tracking. 
- Zebra-free clean white rows separated by hairline `#f0ebe1` dividing rules. 
- Status columns display pill-shaped indicators: Delivered/Active (soft green fill `#dcfce7`, text `#16a34a`), Processing/Pending (amber fill `#fef3c7`, text `#b45309`), and Cancelled (red fill `#fee2e2`, text `#dc2626`).
